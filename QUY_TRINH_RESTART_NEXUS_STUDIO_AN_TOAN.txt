QUY TRINH RESTART NEXUS STUDIO ENGINE AN TOAN
==============================================

Pham vi: E:\yt\Tool\Nexus_Studio_Engine

1. Chay test, lint va build phu hop voi thay doi.
2. Kiem tra GET http://127.0.0.1:8080/api/system/maintenance-status.
3. Chi tiep tuc khi safe_to_restart=true va total_blocking_jobs=0.
4. Neu endpoint khong phan hoi, chay guard canonical:

   $env:PYTHONPATH = "$PWD\src"
   .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m nexus_engine.services.maintenance_guard

   Neu khong xac minh duoc idle thi coi la busy va KHONG restart.

5. Restart bang duy nhat entrypoint:

   E:\yt\Tool\Nexus_Studio_Engine\run_nexus_restart.bat

6. Xac minh ba core service:

   - Backend API: http://127.0.0.1:8080
   - Frontend UI: http://127.0.0.1:5173
   - OmniVoice: http://127.0.0.1:8011

ChatGPT va Google Flow la dich vu on-demand. Startup khong duoc tu mo GPM,
profile ChatGPT, Google Flow, scanner, comment job hoac channel job.

Khong taskkill hang loat python.exe, node.exe hoac chrome.exe. Stop chi duoc
dung ownership marker/command line cua du an va khong dong browser ca nhan.
